How to play

Goal. Get the ball ○ into the hole ●. Par is 6 on every hole; the fewest total strokes wins.

Dice golf. Roll the die to see how far the ball travels. Add 1 when hitting from the fairway, subtract 1 when hitting from sand. Then pick one of the 8 directions and the ball flies that many dots in a straight line.

Speed golf. No die: choose a club instead. Driver moves 6 (fairway only, can fly over trees), iron moves 3 (2 from sand, never over trees), putter moves 1.

Terrain. Trees block your shot and can never be landed on — but a shot struck from the fairway sails over them. Water can be flown over, never landed in. Sand shortens your next hit. Slope arrows roll the ball one extra dot (and keep rolling across chained arrows).

Sinking it. Land exactly on the hole and it drops. If your line crosses the hole and finishes just one dot past it, that counts too.

Do-overs. Your first shot on each hole may be re-rolled once for free. Beyond that, each player carries 6 mulligans for the whole course — spend one to re-roll any shot. You may always choose to putt (move 1) instead of using your roll.

Multiplayer. Everyone tees off in order; after that, whoever sits furthest from the hole shoots next. Keep an eye out — a bigfoot hides somewhere in about a third of all notebooks, and spotting him earns a bonus mulligan.
